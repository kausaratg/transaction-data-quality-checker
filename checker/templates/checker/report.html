Processing performed

All records were retained in the downloadable CSV. Each record includes its validation status and an explanation of any issues found.

  1. Recognised status values were standardised to Success, Failed, or Pending, ignoring capitalisation and surrounding spaces.
  2. Valid day/month/year dates were formatted as DD/MM/YYYY. Invalid or missing dates were flagged.
  3. Required fields were checked for missing values.
  4. Transaction IDs were checked for duplicates. All occurrences of repeated IDs were flagged.
  5. Amounts were checked for numeric, finite values greater than zero.
  6. Every record was labelled Passed or Needs review.
